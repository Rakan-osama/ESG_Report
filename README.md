## Overview

A repository associated with work on **Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG)** and sustainability reporting. My related professional experience includes developing an ESG module on a SaaS platform, concentrating on collecting, validating and organizing data for structured reporting workflows.

The reporting domain references standards such as **GRI (Global Reporting Initiative)**. This describes the broader work area; it does **not** represent a claim that the public repository implements or is certified against the entire GRI framework.

## Domain understanding

ESG reporting brings together three complementary lenses:

| Pillar | Example reporting subjects |
| --- | --- |
| **Environmental** | Energy, emissions, water, waste and resource use |
| **Social** | Employees, safety, diversity and community impacts |
| **Governance** | Ethics, oversight, policies and compliance |

The engineering challenge is to organize heterogeneous business information into **consistent, reviewable, traceable and reusable records**.

## Engineering areas

- Structured data intake and validation.
- Organizing reporting indicators, periods and organizational information.
- Business-rule implementation and coherent reporting workflows.
- Handling reporting completeness and data consistency.
- Connecting domain requirements to maintainable backend logic.
